# # Ritam

### AI Medication Companion for Evidence-Grounded Drug Education

Ritam is an AI medication companion that transforms complex official drug documentation into simple, conversational, and evidence-grounded guidance for patients and caregivers.

Instead of generating answers from general medical knowledge, Ritam retrieves information from approved drug documents and uses an LLM to explain that information in simpler language.

> **No evidence → No answer.**

---

## Why Ritam?

Official medication documents contain important information, but they are often written using technical medical and regulatory language that can be difficult for patients and caregivers to understand.

Ritam acts as a communication layer between the official medication document and the person taking the medicine.
